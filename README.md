# Tool Share

### Intro:
This app is designed to bring the neighborhood together in the spirit of mutual aid. 

We don't all need to own a prescision screwdriver or pricey carpet cleaners. That's why we've got our neighbors! 

Let's use this app to engage in reciprocity and try out fun home tools with a trusted community. The neighborhood is what you make of it and with our built in authentication and login features, neighbors can keep each other accountable for the tools they are borrowing. 

Your friendly neighborhood tool library!

### Features:
- Start off with a log in or sign up page so you can share your items to lend or borrow items in the inventory.
- Use the 'Add a tool to the library' form to share some details about the tool.
- Read what your neighbors have to lend and give a tool a try! 
- To borrow items, click on the 'borrow' button and add your name and the date you plan to return the item.
- Lenders can delete tools when they are no longer available to borrow.
